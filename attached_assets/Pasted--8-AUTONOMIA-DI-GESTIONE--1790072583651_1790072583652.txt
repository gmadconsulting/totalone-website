8. AUTONOMIA DI GESTIONE
==================================================

This is an important differentiating section.

The key concept is NOT that additional users or showrooms are necessarily free.

The key concept is:

Totalone is designed to let customers independently manage the normal evolution of their organization without requiring Totalone staff to manually make those changes for them.

Do NOT confuse:
- subscription pricing
with
- intervention / modification fees.

The subscription price may vary depending on the customer's configuration or commercial agreement.

However, Totalone does NOT charge a technical intervention fee simply because the customer needs to perform a normal administrative change that can be managed directly inside the platform.

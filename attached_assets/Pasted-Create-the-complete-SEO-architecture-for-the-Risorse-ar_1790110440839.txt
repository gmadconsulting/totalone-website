==================================================

Commercial / transactional intent.

Educational / consideration intent.

“Come scegliere e utilizzare un CRM per uno showroom di arredamento”

BLOG ARTICLE:

Example:
“Come registrare e analizzare gli ingressi in showroom”

Do NOT create Blog articles whose title, H1 and search intent simply duplicate existing Totalone product pages.

Product pages must remain the strongest commercial destination.

==================================================

Do not optimize Totalone for generic furniture or interior-design traffic.

The objective is to build topical authority specifically around:

HOW TO RUN, ORGANIZE AND DIGITALIZE A FURNITURE SHOWROOM OR FURNITURE RETAIL BUSINESS.

Every piece of content should therefore have a logical relationship with the problems Totalone actually solves.

The desired journey is:

SEARCH
→
LEARN
→
UNDERSTAND THE PROCESS
→
DISCOVER THE RELEVANT TOTALONE MODULE
→
BOOK A DEMO

Do not change existing product functionality or existing product-page copy while implementing this architecture.
